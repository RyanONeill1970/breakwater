## Exact prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: brainstorming concept illustration for the existing indie tank tactics game Breakwater, a modern reimagining of Pacific Islands (1992). This is proposed art direction, not a screenshot or an implemented game. Primary request: Show how a small tropical tank battlefield can become beautiful, tactically readable and exciting. Wide landscape high oblique isometric gameplay-like camera, one cohesive scene, entire engagement visible. A believable compact irregular Pacific island coastline with turquoise shallow water and darker ocean, pale sand, muted olive grass, clustered palms along a jungle edge, a basalt outcrop that blocks the centre, small radio station on the north, a simple port and seawall, a winding exposed coastal road and an alternative sheltered inland route around the rock. Several clearly visible and recognizable contemporary tanks in two distinct friendly groups: one group holding a road approach behind a low wall, second group moving around the side of the rock to outflank a pair of opposing tanks near the radio. Broad clean shapes, lightly painterly stylized 3D diorama, economical indie-game geometry, convincing grounded vehicles large enough to read, consistent material treatment, restrained surface detail, soft cool shadow masses, warm late-afternoon light, excellent visual hierarchy, calm sea and uncluttered ground. A localized smoke screen obscures only one crossing and must not obscure the main battle. A short dusty trail reveals the flanking group's movement. One small muzzle flash and one dark damaged vehicle tell the action clearly. No mountains you can drive up, playable roads mostly level. Avoid a square island, huge cliffs, excessive tiny props, photorealistic noisy texture, overexposure, giant explosions, sci-fi lasers, laser route lines, bloom haze covering the view, photorealism, HUD, UI panels, text, logos, watermark. The visual goal is readable terrain + visible coordinated movement + a distinctive tropical miniature war scene.
